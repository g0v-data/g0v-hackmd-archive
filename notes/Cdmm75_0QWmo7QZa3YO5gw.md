# [Intro] WaytoSafety 隨時隨地知道避難場所位置 Shelter Map (Taiwan, Japan, Korea, HK) 

https://g0v.hackmd.io/@waytosafety/home

---

Here’s a concise 3-minute English script you could use as a talk:

[Opening]
- Hello everyone, today I’d like to introduce WaytoSafety, a civic tech project that helps people know where to find the nearest evacuation shelter — anytime, anywhere.

[Background & Problem]
- In many places, especially in East Asia, we face risks from earthquakes, typhoons, floods, and other disasters. When an emergency happens, every second counts. 
- However, people often don’t know where to go, or the information about shelters is scattered, inconsistent, or not easy to access.

[[Ongoing] What WaytoSafety Does]
- WaytoSafety builds a Shelter Map that currently covers Taiwan, Japan, Korea, and Hong Kong. 
- It gathers official open data released by governments, then standardizes and visualizes this information so anyone can quickly find the nearest evacuation site. 
- The project want to integrates geospatial data, provides simple map-based browsing, and works across devices.

[Collaboration & Future]
- WaytoSafety started within the g0v community, and it remains open-source. This means developers, designers, and local communities can collaborate — whether by improving the interface, adding more datasets, or connecting to emergency alert systems. 
- The future vision is to expand the coverage, keep datasets updated, and strengthen disaster resilience through digital tools.
