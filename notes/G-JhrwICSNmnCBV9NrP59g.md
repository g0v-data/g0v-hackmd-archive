<p>Minimum deposit is a simple phrase. It denotes the smallest amount required to fund an account so wagering can begin.</p>

<p>Sites use it for two related steps: first funding the wallet so an account balance shows as available, and second qualifying for certain promotions or wagering thresholds where operators attach separate conditions that may require higher stakes or larger deposits to unlock bonuses or free spins.</p>

<p>Operators must state the amount in their terms and payment pages. Players see it before they confirm a payment in most cases, and the figure often varies by payment method and by the specific product that the operator is offering on its platform.</p>
