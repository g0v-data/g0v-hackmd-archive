## Why Choosing the Right Rug Matters

A rug can become one of the main visual elements in a room. It can introduce colour, add texture, define an area, and create a warmer atmosphere around existing furniture.

However, a rug that looks attractive on its own may not necessarily be the right choice for a particular room. Its size may not suit the furniture layout, its pile may not be practical for the space, or its cleaning requirements may not match your lifestyle.

Considering these factors before buying can help you find a rug that is both attractive and useful.

## Start with the Room

The first thing to consider is where the rug will be placed. Different rooms experience different levels of activity and have different practical requirements.

A living room may benefit from a large statement rug that connects the seating area. A bedroom may call for a softer design that feels comfortable underfoot. A kitchen or hallway, meanwhile, may require something easier to maintain because these areas often experience more regular traffic.

Thinking about the purpose of the room gives you a useful starting point when exploring different rug styles.

## Think Carefully About Rug Size

Size is one of the most important factors when choosing a rug. A rug that is too small can make furniture arrangements feel disconnected, while a rug that is too large may overwhelm the available space.

Before choosing a size, measure the area where you want the rug to sit. Also consider your furniture placement.

For example, in a living room, you may want the rug to sit beneath the front legs of your sofa and chairs. In a bedroom, you may prefer a larger rug around the bed or smaller rugs positioned beside it.

The **Renoazul** collection includes different sizes, from smaller rugs and doormats to medium, large, and extra-large options, as well as round rugs and hallway runners. This gives homeowners more flexibility when working with different room layouts.

## Choose a Rug Based on Comfort

Comfort is another important consideration, particularly in rooms where you spend a lot of time.

High-pile and shaggy rugs can provide a soft, cosy feeling that works well in bedrooms and relaxing living spaces. Low-pile rugs can offer a cleaner profile and may be more practical for areas where furniture, doors, or frequent foot traffic need to be considered.

Think about how you want the rug to feel underfoot as well as how you want it to look.
