Before interviewing anywhere lately I check the company’s Instagram stories to get a read on the actual culture before showing up. Job listings and websites are too polished to be genuinely useful. [InstaStoryViewer](https://instastoryviewer.com.co) handles this without any account needed, just enter the handle and the stories load in your browser in seconds. Good for getting a sense of team size, office environment, how people actually interact day to day. Saves surprises and gives you better and more specific questions to bring into the conversation. Has become a regular part of how I research places before applying now
