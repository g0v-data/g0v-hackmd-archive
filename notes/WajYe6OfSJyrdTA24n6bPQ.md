<h2>What house edge means</h2>
<p>House edge is the percentage of every bet that the operator expects to keep over the long run. That&rsquo;s the simple part. If a game has a house edge of 2 percent, the house expects to keep, on average, &pound;2 for every &pound;100 staked, over many plays.</p>
<p>It&rsquo;s not a promise about one spin or one visit. It&rsquo;s a statistical tilt. You can win big in a single session. You can lose everything in ten spins. The edge only makes sense across thousands of bets.</p>

<p>UK-facing sites often show RTP, short for return to player, instead of naming a house edge. RTP is the flip side of the house edge. A game with 97 percent RTP has a 3 percent house edge. Simple maths, but the presentation matters to players.</p>
<p>Operators report figures differently in public filings. The Gambling Commission&rsquo;s online operator data release for April 2025 to March 2026 is the latest industry statistics set in the gathered sources, and it uses Gross Gambling Yield to show actual revenue across products.</p>
