An Ethereum paper wallet is a secure, offline method of storing Ethereum (ETH) and ERC-20 tokens by printing your public address and private key on a physical piece of paper. This cold storage approach keeps your private keys completely disconnected from the internet, protecting them from online hacks, malware, and phishing attacks. Ethereum paper wallets are ideal for long-term holders who do not need frequent access to their funds. They are free to create and give you full control over your assets. https://paperwalleth.com/ is a user-friendly tool that generates safe and clean Ethereum paper wallets with clear layouts and QR codes for easy use.
