<p>A uk casino welcome bonus is a promotional tool designed to attract new customers. The most common type matches a percentage of your first deposit. <p>It comes with a wagering requirement, often called "playthrough". This is the rule that catches many people out. <h2>Why is the Wagering Requirement so Crucial?</h2>
<p>High wagering requirements are the main reason a welcome bonus can be poor value. They are designed to protect the casino's money, making it statistically very hard for you to turn the bonus into withdrawable profit. For a budget player, a high multiplier on a small bonus can still mean an impossibly large sum to wager. You could easily burn through your original deposit trying to meet the target.</p>
<p>Some games contribute differently towards these requirements. Slots usually count 100%, but table games like blackjack or roulette might only contribute 10% or even less. This means if you play a game with a 10% contribution rate, only 10 pence of every £1 you bet goes towards clearing the wagering. It makes meeting the target take ten times longer! Always find the game contribution table in the bonus terms.</p>
